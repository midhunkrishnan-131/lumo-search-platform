Security scanning principles

Honest Verdicts LUMO never fabricates scan results. Without the FastAPI scanner connected, remote risk always reports as Unknown.
Risk Scale Low Risk · Suspicious · Malicious · Unknown (Low Risk is never an absolute guarantee of safety).
SSRF & Safety Limits Blocks private/loopback networks, enforces strict redirect and response-size caps on the backend.

Analyse suspicious link

Checks URL structure, scheme security, internationalized Punycode (xn--) lookalikes, obfuscated credentials, and SSRF rules locally, then routes to FastAPI POST /api/v1/guardian/url when connected.
